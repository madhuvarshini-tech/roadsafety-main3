Tamil Nadu Safety & Compliance Initiative

About Us

Dedicated to raising workplace safety standards across Tamil Nadu.

Road Minded Safety Solutions leads the Tamil Nadu Safety & Compliance Initiative — delivering practical workplace safety workshops for factories, construction sites, offices, and industrial facilities across the state.

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, high-quality safety training that empowers workers, safety officers, and organizations to create safer workplaces across Tamil Nadu.

Our Vision

A Tamil Nadu where every workplace prioritizes safety, compliance, and the well-being of its workforce through continuous education and awareness.

Our Approach

We combine practical hands-on training, industry-relevant curriculum, and experienced trainers to deliver workshops that meet real-world safety challenges in factories, construction sites, and offices.

Training Coverage

Workshops are conducted across Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, and major Tamil Nadu industrial hubs, with in-plant training available on request.
